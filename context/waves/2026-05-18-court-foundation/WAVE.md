# Wave: COURT Foundation

## Goal

Create COURT as the scalable experience framework foundation and prove the first
portable snapshot/action/scene contract.

## Thesis

The same product state should be able to feed terminal, browser, native, and
future authored-scene engines. COURT starts by naming that boundary without
owning product rules or engine rendering.
