# COURT Research

Use this skill before broad COURT design, benchmark, or adapter decisions.

## Research requirements

- Compare at least two existing engines or frameworks when choosing an adapter.
- Separate product-rule ownership from renderer/input/tooling ownership.
- Record citations and decision criteria.
- Do not promote a dependency until a product or engine adapter proves the need.
