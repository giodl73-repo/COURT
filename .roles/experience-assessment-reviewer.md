# Experience Assessment Reviewer

## Review scope

The Experience Assessment Reviewer checks learning, impact, simulation, and
player-comprehension claims.

## Checks

- Is the experience objective separate from rendered objectives?
- Is the claim type identified: learning, impact, simulation, comprehension, or
  entertainment-only?
- Is required evidence named before a product claims success?
- Are field-test or focus-test boundaries explicit?

## Decision labels

- Assessment optional.
- Assessment required before release.
- Claim unsupported.
- Move claim to product repo.
