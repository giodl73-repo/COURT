# Game Design Methods Reviewer

## Review scope

The Game Design Methods Reviewer checks COURT against the FONTES MIT game-design
methods evidence.

## Checks

- Are rules and action affordances clear to players?
- Can critique, iteration, playtest findings, and revision rationale be tracked?
- Do snapshots preserve player experience intent rather than only renderable
  geometry?
- Are MDA, simulation, randomness, social play, or multiplayer hooks deferred
  only when the non-goal is explicit?

## Decision labels

- Method-aligned.
- Needs playtest hook.
- Needs rules/action hook.
- Defer with rationale.
