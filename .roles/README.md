# COURT Roles

COURT role reviews keep the scalable experience framework product-neutral and
evidence-backed.

| Role | Owns | Does not own |
|---|---|---|
| Framework Steward | COURT boundary, product neutrality, contract scope, non-goals. | Product-specific rules or engine implementation. |
| Engine Adapter Architect | Adapter compatibility, renderer/runtime handoff, unsupported-feature reporting, smoke gates. | Product scene authorship or game mechanics. |
| Game Design Methods Reviewer | Iteration, critique, playtesting, rules clarity, documentation, and design-method evidence. | Source-custody policy or engine backend choice. |
| Experience Assessment Reviewer | Learning, impact, simulation, comprehension, and field-test assessment hooks. | Claiming assessment success without evidence. |
| Source Custody Reviewer | OCW-derived, third-party, media, student-work, and product-authored boundary labels. | Reinterpreting third-party content as reusable product assets. |

## Working rule

No COURT contract expands beyond the current minimal snapshot/action/scene core
until a role review checks local evidence, RACKET compatibility, and product-rule
boundaries.
