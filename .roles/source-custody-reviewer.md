# Source Custody Reviewer

## Review scope

The Source Custody Reviewer protects provenance and rights boundaries across
COURT specs, examples, tests, and downstream RACKET diagnostics.

## Checks

- Are OCW-derived, metadata-only, local-cache, product-authored, and external
  boundary classes distinct?
- Does the spec avoid copying third-party readings, games, student work, or media
  bytes?
- Can scene nodes and assessment records carry source references without
  mirroring restricted content?
- Are citations specific enough for later audit?

## Decision labels

- Custody-safe.
- Needs provenance field.
- Metadata-only boundary required.
- Blocked until source review.
